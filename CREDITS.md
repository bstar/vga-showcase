# Credits

The real-mode DOS slideshow player, conversion tools and packaging are this
project's work, copyright 2026 bstar, licensed under MIT.

The 32 archived GIF images retain their original authorship and ownership;
they are not relicensed as original MIT artwork. They were collected from the
MIT Athena picture archive, including its historical `OldFiles/dispaly`
directory. Archive dates are source records, not claims about creation dates.

Each image's original filename, archive URL, recorded date, dimensions and
SHA-256 checksum are in [images/sources.json](images/sources.json). The DOS ZIP
includes byte-for-byte copies under `GIFS` and a readable source list in
`IMAGES.TXT`. The README screenshot shows `waterfall.gif` in the slideshow.

This is a new slideshow using archived images, not a recovered manufacturer's
demo disk. The repository and release do not include VPIC or other historical
viewers researched alongside the original demo.

Open Watcom, DOSBox-X and ImageMagick are separate build/test tools and retain
their own licenses; their executables are not bundled in the DOS release.
